Care for Creation & Social Responsibility

At Assumption College of Davao (ACD), caring for creation requires responsible stewardship of food and natural resources. Avoiding food wastage, practicing waste segregation, and sharing surplus food mobilizes compassion, environmental attentiveness, and social responsibility across our campus and broader community.

🤝

Target Audience Directories

Action Guides by Role

🎓

Student Guide

Save food every day

Request sensible portions in cafeterias, store dorm snacks in airtight containers, and freeze extra leftovers.

💡 Practical Tip: Plan meals before buying snacks to prevent spoilage!
👨‍🍳

Kitchen Staff Guide

Make every ingredient count

Practice FIFO (First In, First Out) inventory rotation, prepare in batch intervals, and reduce prep trimmings.

💡 Practical Tip: Use vegetable trimmings and bones for rich stocks!
🧹

Janitor / Ops Guide

Keep campus clean & sorted

Maintain clear 4-stream waste segregation bins (Biodegradable, Recyclable, Residual, Special Waste).

💡 Practical Tip: Ensure food waste goes directly to compost bins!
🤝

Community Guide

Share resources responsibly

Donate unopened non-perishable canned items and whole fruits to community fridges and pantries.

💡 Practical Tip: Always verify food safety before donating!
🧊

Food Storage Best Practices

Store foods in optimal temperature zones to extend usable shelf life:

  • Pantry: Cool, dry cabinets for potatoes & onions (store separately!).
  • Refrigerator: Keep under 4°C (40°F) for dairy, cooked meals, and meats.
  • Freezer: Freeze bread slices, soups, and chopped produce for long-term safety.
📅

Decoding Date Labels

Understand what expiration labels actually mean:

  • Best If Used By: Indicates peak flavor & quality (NOT a safety deadline).
  • Use By: Manufacturer quality recommendation. Evaluated visually/by smell.
  • Sell By: Retail inventory rotation date for store staff.
